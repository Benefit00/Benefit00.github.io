--- title: Embed the blog kicker: For the company website description: "Show the latest posts on any web page with two lines of HTML." permalink: /embed/ ---

Paste this where the posts should appear on the company website. It loads in isolation, so it won't clash with the site's own styling, and it updates automatically whenever a new post is published.

<div data-benefit-blog data-limit="3"></div>
<script src="{{ '/embed.js' | absolute_url }}" async></script>
